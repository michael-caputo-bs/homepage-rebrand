The Methodology

Inside the market aggregate model.

Our market aggregate is built on four interlocking principles — the same principles that power the PRO Odds Screen trusted by professional trading syndicates. Each one addresses a specific structural weakness in conventional prop pricing.

01

Historical book-efficiency weighting

Each book is weighted by sport, market, and prop type based on its historical accuracy. Pinnacle's NBA spread isn't the same signal as their NFL alts — and the model treats them differently, weighting the most efficient book on each market the highest.

02

Bias correction by prop type

Anytime TD prices differently than first TD. Receiving-yards ladders behave differently from passing-yards lines. The model corrects per prop type rather than treating all props as one bucket — pricing each market on its own distribution instead of a single football-wide model.

03

Statistical outlier exclusion

Before a price is set, book quotes that fall outside the expected distribution are down-weighted or excluded under a published, auditable rule. A single mispriced or lagging book can't drag the aggregate — the consensus stays anchored to the books you trust.

04

Backtesting against closing prices

Every price is graded against the closing line on the same market across 5+ seasons of history. Book weights and bias corrections are recalibrated continuously against those closing-price outcomes, so the model is measured on realized accuracy rather than opinion.

FAQ

Built for the questions
trading desks actually ask.

What is the Sportsbook Pricing Feed?
A market-weighted consensus price for North American player props — a single, most-accurate reference blended from the wider market and delivered as an API built to run inside your own trading stack. (See coverage below for the full sport and market list.)
What sports, markets, and books do you cover?
NFL, NBA, MLB, NCAAF, NCAAB, NHL, and UFC — player props, alternate lines, ladders, and milestones, priced across 200+ books. Each market is modeled on its own rather than stretched from a single general model. If there's a specific market you need that isn't listed, tell us — coverage is scoped to your book.
Can I configure the pricing to my book?
Yes. Weightings are configurable by book, market, and prop type, with bulk changes across many props at once, and you set your own margins. Use our aggregate as the reference where you want it and your own model where you don't — you keep full control of the price you publish.
How do you keep pricing accurate and protect against bad data?
Every price is graded against the closing line on the same market across 5+ seasons and recalibrated continuously. Book quotes that fall outside the expected distribution are down-weighted or excluded under a published, auditable rule, so a single lagging or mispriced book can't drag the aggregate — and flagged markets surface on an outlier alert feed.
How fast can we integrate, and can we run it alongside our current feed?
The feed delivers as REST or WebSocket in JSON, with a polling fallback, and is built to drop into your existing infrastructure. Most teams run us alongside their current provider first as a fallback reference, compare over a season, and promote us to primary once we win the trade study. A dedicated integration engineer and a shared Slack channel support you through launch and beyond.
Do you provide historical odds, and how do markets map to our system?
Yes — full tick history, every book, live markets included, on the same consistent IDs as the live API, so markets map one-to-one with no fuzzy matching. You can reconstruct the full price path on any market for backtesting or retrospective account review. Want to try it? We'll set you up with a trial key alongside your current feed.
